# Lore of the Dustline

The story behind SCDMG: the world, the war, the three sides and the places they fight over. In the game, open **📖 Lore** from the main menu (or **Read their story ›** under an army, or **📖 Lore** on a built-in faction in the Faction forge).

The canonical version lives in code, in [`src/engine/data/lore.ts`](../src/engine/data/lore.ts), so the lore pages, the intro animations and the campaign all read from the same place. `tests/lore.test.ts` checks that it only names units, supports, maps and missions that exist, and that this page mentions every character, place and battle. If you change one, change the other.

## The Dustline

Out where the paved road gives up and the corn does not, there is a long, dry farming and mining valley called the Dustline. A fizzing creek runs down the middle of it, the soil is suspiciously good, the pumpkins are enormous, and the pies are the best for three hundred miles. Nobody asks too many questions about any of this, because the answer is under the ford.

### The Fizz and the Wellspring

Under the valley runs the Fizz: bubbling, faintly glowing mineral water that rises through the Fizzwater creek. It makes crops grow like they have somewhere to be, and it keeps forty-year-old engines running on fumes and spite. The Muster pours it in its war machines. The Skitterswarm sleeps curled around it for the warmth. The Lumen Concord refine it into light. Its source, the Wellspring, lies deep under Fizzwater Ford, and whoever holds the Wellspring holds the Dustline. (The fizz you spend on support cards is a little of the same stuff.)

### The Dustline War

This season, the valley is under attack from two directions. From below, the Skitterswarm is boiling up through the fields, every bug of it answering to Mother Mandible, who means to lay the Great Clutch in the warmth of the Wellspring. From above, the Lumen Concord have parked the Star Lens in orbit and begun landscaping the valley into a tasteful pleasure garden for His Radiance, with the Wellspring as its fountain. In between stands the Dustline Muster: every family's hands, drafted, drilled and bolted into riveted armour. Three sides, one valley, and nobody is leaving.

### How we got here

| When | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| Generations ago | **The Settling.** Wagons of settlers followed the creek into the valley, noticed the water fizzed, decided that was probably fine, and stayed. They built windpumps, a Grange Hall and a very long fence, and started arguing about whose pie was best. The argument continues. |
| The boom years | **The Fizz Boom.** Fizz ran a tractor better than diesel, and the valley boomed. The Old Refinery bottled it, the mines went deep after the richer seams, the Long Ridge Highway hauled it out, and ore trains rattled past Gutterbend. Then the shallow seam went flat, the refinery closed, a train came off the rails and stayed off, and everyone went back to farming, with a great deal of interesting scrap and a few shafts that went deeper than anybody liked to talk about. |
| Some years back | **Something Below.** The deep shafts were capped and the miners stopped talking about the clicking. The wells ran warm, and dogs wouldn't go near Badger Knob. Down around the Wellspring, something very old and very hungry had been woken by the drilling, and it was laying eggs. |
| The Harvest of '09 | **Up Through the Corn.** The first Skitterlings came up through the corn. The farmers held them at Dustbowl Crossing with shovels, a combine and a great deal of shouting. That winter the Grange Hall raised the Dustline Muster: every family sends its hands, the mining work-suits were riveted into armour at the Scrapyard, and squads drilled on the Grange Hall green. Big Bertha got legs and plating. Corporal Squinter has not blinked since. |
| Last autumn | **The Glow in the Dark.** From orbit, the Fizz shows up as a soft glow. The Concord pleasure yacht Velvet Comet came down to admire it and planted itself in a debris field. Its passengers walked away unhurt and deeply offended, and sent a message home: we have found something lovely, and it is surrounded by farmers. |
| This season | **The Dustline War.** The Star Lens has moved into orbit above the valley. Lady Prismwick has replaced the Haybright bean farm with a croquet lawn. The bean rows that are left have started to tremble, and the clicking is back. Marshal Haybright has called the full Muster to the Grange Hall, and attendance is compulsory. |

## The three sides

### Dustline Militia: the frontier, drafted

*Rivets, rifles and a very long memory.*

The Dustline Militia used to be every family in the valley, a radio and a scrapyard. After the Harvest of '09 that stopped being enough, so the Grange Hall raised **the Dustline Muster**. Every family sends its hands: farmhands, ranch kids and mine crews, drafted, drilled on the Grange Hall green and given squad numbers. Their old mining and farm work-suits went to the Scrapyard Sprawl and came back riveted into armour. Rivet Grunts hold the line, Patch Medics keep it standing, and the Old Guard Veterans, recalled from the last two wars, show everyone how a trench is dug.

Their war machines are farm and mine kit, refitted and numbered. Big Bertha is the Haybright combine on legs, with plate armour, a crew of three and a cannon. The Sky Rustlers are crop dusters refitted for ground attack. The Haymaker Bomber drops mine buckets of blasting powder, and the Rust Bucket Drop-Ship is older than most of its passengers. They are not fast, and they do not like getting close, but dig a section in behind a fence with a clear field of fire and it will hold until relieved.

- **Leader: Marshal Dot Haybright**, Marshal of the Dustline Muster. She chaired the Grange Hall committee until the night of '09, raised the Muster that winter and has commanded it from the map table ever since. The troops still call her Aunt Dot, never to her face. It was her bean farm Lady Prismwick turned into a lawn. *"This is Grange Hall Command. Every section, to the Crossing. Rifles loaded, plates riveted, and nobody falls back without my say-so."*
- **Flight Sergeant Lew**, pilot of the Rust Bucket Drop-Ship and ranking flyer of the Forward Airstrip. Everyone still calls him Lucky. He swears he has done every landing before.
- **Corporal "Squinter"**, lead marksman of the Ridgeline Marksmen on Mesa Ridge. Nobody remembers their real name, including them.
- **Big Bertha**, the Haybright family combine, refitted as a walker-tank.
- **Wants:** to hold the valley, bring the harvest in, get Haybright Farm back from the Concord, push the swarm back underground, and keep the Wellspring flowing for the people who were here first.
- **At stake:** the farms, the mines, the Grange Hall, the Harvest Fair and three generations of pie recipes. If the Muster breaks, the Dustline becomes either a lawn or a nest.
- **Home:** the Grange Hall, now the Muster's headquarters.

### The Skitterswarm: the ground is hungry

*Every one of them is hers.*

The Skitterswarm was here first. Long before the wagons came, Mother Mandible slept curled around the Wellspring in the warm dark under the valley, with her brood asleep around her. Then the boom years' mines went deep after the richer seams, and the drills went a little too deep. The clicking started under Badger Knob, and in the Harvest of '09 the first Skitterlings came up through the corn.

Now the Brood Hollow is full to the ceiling with glowing eggs, and Mother Mandible wants more room. Every bug in the swarm hatched from one of her eggs and hears her clicking through the ground: Skitterlings by the hundred, Acid Spitters, Shellback Brood, Trapjaw Lurkers waiting under rocks, Stinger Swarms overhead, and Old Grinder, who dug the Undertunnels in the first place. Acid softens whatever is in the way, the brood pours in after it, and whatever the Muster squashes, she lays again. Where she goes, the swarm stays thick. Where she falls, it comes apart.

- **Leader: Mother Mandible**, the Broodmother. Mostly abdomen, entirely in charge. She doesn't speak human; the Muster's listening posts write down what they can. *"\*click-click\* The ground is warm. The ground is ours."*
- **Old Grinder**, the Deep Borer: the first and biggest of the brood, a tunnelling bug as long as an ore train.
- **Wants:** to reach the Wellspring and lay the Great Clutch in its warmth, so that the whole valley hatches.
- **At stake:** the brood itself. The Wellspring's warmth keeps the eggs alive; lose it, and the Brood Hollow goes cold.
- **Home:** The Brood Hollow, under Badger Knob.

### Lumen Concord: invaders from beyond the Star Lens

*Few, bright and insufferable.*

The Lumen Concord are a very old, very bright civilisation that has toured the stars for so long it has run out of things to admire. When the Star Lens picked up the valley's glow, His Radiance sent the pleasure yacht Velvet Comet to take a look. It crashed. So His Radiance came in person. The Star Lens now hangs over the valley like a monocle, saucers glide down through Light Gates, and Lady Prismwick has already replaced Haybright Farm's bean fields with a croquet lawn.

The Concord are few, and every one of them is a specialist. They do not consider this an invasion. They consider it a renovation. The locals are welcome to stay, provided they keep off the lawn, and the bugs are not welcome at all.

- **Leader: His Radiance, the Grand Strider**, Supreme Luminary of the Lumen Concord, on three gleaming legs. He believes he is the main character. He is, unfortunately, correct. *"Do not be alarmed. You are merely being improved."*
- **Lady Prismwick**, Keeper of Lawns and chair of the Court of Disdain. They would like the Muster to stop standing on the grass.
- **Wants:** to turn the Dustline into a pleasure garden worthy of His Radiance: terraced, hedged, lit from beneath by the Wellspring, and free of farmers, insects and anything beige.
- **At stake:** their saucers, gates and shields run on refined light, and the Wellspring is the brightest source in a thousand worlds. More importantly, losing to a valley of farmers would be embarrassing, and losing to bugs would be worse.
- **Home:** The Star Lens, with His Radiance's flagship, the Bright Palace, beside it.

## The Dustline War, battle by battle

A suggested order for the war, each with a battlefield and mission from the game. For role missions the first side plays side A.

**Act I: The Muster**

1. **Up Through the Corn** (Militia vs Skitterswarm; Dustbowl Crossing, Hold the Line). The first Skitterlings of the season come up through the corn. Hold the silo deck until the rest of the Muster gets there.
2. **Keep Off the Grass** (Militia vs Concord; Lady Prismwick's Lawn, Smash and Grab). Take Haybright Farm back, one hedge at a time.
3. **Signal Lost** (Skitterswarm vs Militia; Rattlevane Junction, Top of the Heap). The swarm has chewed through the radio cables.

**Act II: The War Spreads**

4. **Last Drops** (Skitterswarm vs Concord; Old Refinery, Scrap Rush). There is still warm Fizz in the tanks.
5. **Highway Robbery** (Militia vs Skitterswarm; Long Ridge Highway, The Great Snack Heist). The swarm has dragged off a Muster convoy's crate of Fizz cells.
6. **Finders Keepers** (Militia vs Concord; Wreck of the Velvet Comet, Salvage Rights). The yacht's owners have come back for it, and they are livid.
7. **The Gemhaul Road** (Concord vs Militia; Gemhaul Ambush, Strongbox Run). A crystal caravan has stalled, and the Tumbleweed Outriders have noticed.
8. **Claim Jumpers** (Militia vs Skitterswarm; Grubstake Redoubt, Claim-Jump Siege). A fresh, warm seam of Fizz, and the swarm is not knocking.
9. **Off the Rails** (Skitterswarm vs Concord; Gutterbend Derailment, Shifting Ground). The ground keeps shifting as the swarm tunnels under the ore cars.

**Act III: The Wellspring**

10. **Spare Parts** (Militia vs Skitterswarm; Scrapyard Sprawl, Scrapheap Showdown). The swarm goes for the Muster's armour and spare parts.
11. **Landing Party** (Concord vs Militia; Brinejaw Flats, Frontline). His Radiance's main fleet sets down on the salt.
12. **The Narrows** (Skitterswarm vs Concord; Mesa Ridge, Scrapyard Brawl). Nobody planned this, and nobody is backing down.
13. **The Battle of Fizzwater Ford** (Militia vs Concord; Fizzwater Ford, Hold the Line). Whoever holds the bridges holds the Wellspring, and the Dustline.

## The intros

The intros are drawn live in the game's own style (the real miniatures, emblems and wordmark on inked comic panels); there are no video files. Each one can be skipped with a click, Esc, Space or the Skip button, and with *reduce motion* turned on in your system they show as a still comic page instead.

- **The game intro** (about 19 seconds) never plays by itself; watch it from **▶ Watch the intro** on the main menu or the lore pages: the valley at dawn, the fizzing creek and the Wellspring below it, the Skitterswarm bursting up through the corn, Concord saucers beaming down at night, the Muster standing firm, and the three emblems facing off under the SCDMG wordmark.
- **A faction intro** (about 16 seconds each) plays from **▶ Play the intro** on the faction's lore page:
  - *Dustline Militia:* Marshal Haybright calling the Muster roll, squads in riveted armour falling in, Big Bertha striding out of the motor pool, the crop dusters overhead in formation, and the title card.
  - *The Skitterswarm:* the ground trembling under Badger Knob, the Brood Hollow's eggs glowing, Mother Mandible revealed, the swarm pouring out of the tunnels, and the title card.
  - *Lumen Concord:* the Star Lens noticing the glow, saucers beaming down, His Radiance stepping out of a Light Gate, the Court of Disdain scoring the locals, and the title card.

## The Munch

Until the Skitterswarm took their place, the third side of the war was **the Munch**: Dr. Graft's grafted farmland critters, led by the doctor on his war-badger, Big Mama. They have left the Dustline's story, but not the game: the Munch live on whole (units, support cards, abilities and flavour) as a trope template in the Faction forge, under *Dustline veterans*.

## For campaign builders

The game's own campaign, the Dustline War, is built on this lore: see [campaign.md](campaign.md). Import from `src/engine/data/lore.ts`:

- `WORLD`: `name`, `summary`, `prize`, `conflict`, `history` (eras), `intro` (captions).
- `LORE_FACTIONS[factionId]`: `epithet`, `tagline`, `leaderId`, `homeId`, `goal`, `stakes`, `story`, `quote`, and a lore note for every unit (`units`) and support card (`supports`), keyed by the game's ids.
- `CHARACTERS`: id, name, title, faction, pronoun, and the `unitId` or `supportId` they appear as on the table.
- `PLACES`: id, name, kind, the `mapId` of its battlefield (if any), who holds it when the story starts, and a blurb.
- `BATTLES`: battle seeds in three acts, each with a place, map, mission and the two sides. Tests check every mission fits its map.
- Helpers: `factionLore`, `leaderOf`, `charactersOf`, `characterById`, `placeById`, `placeForMap`, `battlesFor`, `battlesAt`, `loreNote`.
