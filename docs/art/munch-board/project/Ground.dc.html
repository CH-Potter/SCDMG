Farmland, with a spill

The shared team ring stays. Inside it, every Munch base is a patch of the farm they were taken from, with one small sign of the lab. The scatter comes from a stable seed so it never flickers.
Field dirt #8a6b48, light enough that dark coats (beetle, skunk, crow) separate from it.
Plough furrows and a few straw strokes.
One mutagen puddle near the rim, small, faintly glowing: the only bright note.
Pebbles, and on bigger bases a dropped surgical staple.
Keep it quiet. No syringes or tools lying about: the lab touch is a spill and a staple, nothing that competes with the critter.
