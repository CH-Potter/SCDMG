The scientist and his war-badger

The faction's leader and centrepiece. Every other Munch takes orders from the transmitter in his raised hand.
The badger: a huge grey sett-digger with black legs and the striped face. Chrome armour plates are stapled over the shoulder and rump, and steel digging claws are grafted on for Tunnel. It wears the biggest implant of them all.
The scientist: lab coat, wild white hair, green-lit goggles and a manic grin. He holds the overmind transmitter up: a chrome box with a short mast and a flat dish, signal arcs pulsing on the step frame for Brood Call.
The link: mutagen tanks on the saddle, piped into the badger's neck.
Silhouette at board size: a wide grey mass with a white figure and a raised arm on top. No other unit has a rider, so the leader is never mistaken.
