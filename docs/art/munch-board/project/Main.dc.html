Coats stay natural. Flat cel shading, one shadow tone, no lab colour on fur or shell. The animal reads first.
Chrome is clean. Cool, glossy, hard specular, held on with surgical staples and stitches. Never rust or rivets: that is the Militia's junk-tech.
Green means the signal. Implant lenses, hypnotised eye glints, mutagen in glass. It glows, and it is the only thing that does.
