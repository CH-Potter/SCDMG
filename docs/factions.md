# Faction pillars

Each built-in faction has its own way of fighting. Units and supports should fall inside these pillars, with a little deliberate overlap so no faction is helpless in any situation. When you add or change content, check it against this page; `tests/factionIdentity.test.ts` checks the main pillars at the data level.

| | Dustline Militia | The Munch | Lumen Concord |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Play style | Ranged gunline | Melee horde | Few elite specialists |
| Wins by | Out-shooting you from cover | Drowning you in bodies | Precise strikes and being in the right place |
| Weak at | Melee; getting caught in the open | Shooting; nerve; armour | Numbers; holding many objectives; losses hurt |
| Arrival | Drop-ship anywhere | Burrow Hole (temporary) and Warren Mound (permanent) | Grand Portal beside a friend, overcharged |
| Buff family | Ranged dice, digging in, supplies | Melee dice, regrowth, swarming | Shields, precision and tracking, teleporting |

## Dustline Militia (frontier settlers)

**Pillars**
1. **Gunlines.** Rifle squads, snipers and cannons that hit on 4+ at long range and get better when they stand still (Steady Aim) or dig in.
2. **Artillery and turrets.** Barrage, the Spud Mortar, the Scrap Turret: indirect fire and fixed guns.
3. **Overwatch and entrenching.** Overwatch, Entrenched, Sandbag Social (dig in), Junk Barricades, Dust Devil.
4. **Air support and logistics.** The drop-ship, the airstrip, the radio's resources, field repairs.
5. **Grit.** Steadying Presence and Patch Up keep the farmers standing.

**Weak at:** melee (one die per model, hitting on 5+ for most squads), and anything that reaches its gunline. Low armour (5+) without cover.

**Belongs here**
- Keywords: infantry, light, mech; a few fearless defenders.
- Abilities: Overwatch, Entrenched, Steady Aim, Spotter, Barrage, Demo Charge, Patch Up, Steadying Presence, Rocket Hop.
- Supports: resources, heal (repairs, no revives), rangedBuff, dugIn, the ward reaction, airdrops (near your models, or anywhere for the drop-ship), the Junk Barricade summon.
- Stats: hit 4+ (3+ for snipers); range 20–30"; armour 5 (Bertha 3); nerve 7; 1–5 models per unit; cost 2–5. Precision on snipers and the crop duster; tracking on auto-guns.

**Arrival:** the **Rust Bucket Drop-Ship** lands a reserve unit anywhere on the table from round 2, at least 8" from enemies, for just 1 CP. It's the Skirmish starter's arrival card. The **Dirt Airstrip** drops one within 12" of your models for 3 CP, and it's the Assault starter's. On the 70" board the gunline needs it to keep pace: without it the faster Munch reached the centre objectives first and the Militia scored about 15% on the lane maps (Highway, Sprawl). The drop-ship in Assault overshot to about 68%.

**Overlap:** the Front Porch Grannies' rolling pins and Big Bertha's stomp and ram.

## The Munch (critter swarm)

**Pillars**
1. **Horde.** Cheap 1 HP bodies in big squads (5–8 models); the Munch always field the most models.
2. **Impact on the charge.** Most melee critters roll impact dice when a charge lands; Rampage and Feeding Frenzy add more melee dice.
3. **Regrowth.** Regen, Regrow, Mending Spores, Hatchlings and Cough Up: wounds close and lost critters come back.
4. **Tunnels and ambushes.** Burrow Holes, the Warren Mound, Tunnel, Lurk and the Gobble Pit.
5. **Gross stuff.** Sticky Goo, Reel In, Pop!, Awful Whiff (the only nerve attack), Last Bite.

**Weak at:** shooting (nothing past 20"), armour (6+ on most bodies), and nerve (6). It loses lots of models and relies on getting them back.

**Belongs here**
- Keywords: beast, infantry, light; monsters; jumpers and flyers that bite.
- Abilities: Regen, Regrow, Hatchlings, Lurk, Sticky Goo, Rampage, Sugar Rush, Brood Call, Mending Spores, Tunnel, Burrow Exit, Pop!, Reel In.
- Supports: meleeBuff, regrow, rattle, slow, retaliate, and summons (Burrow Hole, Gobble Pit).
- Stats: melee hit 4+ (Big Mama 3+); armour 6 (big beasts 4–5); nerve 6–7; cost 2–6, lowest per model; impact on most melee units. Evade against blasts and point-blank shots on swarms.

**Arrival:** two-tier tunnelling. **Tunnel Network** opens a temporary **Burrow Hole** (gone at the end of the round) that your reserves can deploy next to. The **Warren Mound** is the permanent hole, bought in the list. Big Mama's **Tunnel Claws** let her burrow across the board once.

**Overlap:** the Goo Spitter and the Gloop Lobber shoot, but short-ranged and mostly to slow the enemy with goo so the swarm can catch them.

## Lumen Concord (crystal aristocrats)

**Pillars**
1. **Elite specialists.** Few, expensive models (1–3 per unit), each with one clear job: duellists, a sniper, a flyer, a support caster, a monster.
2. **Shields.** Every Concord unit has shields; Radiance, Refocus, Bubble Shield and Refraction keep them topped up.
3. **Precision and tracking.** Hit on 3+, with precision (land misses, shoot into melee) and tracking (beat evasion). Point Out Flaws adds more.
4. **Light and repositioning.** Blink, Recall Beam, Gate Step and the Grand Portal put the right unit in the right place.
5. **Auras and smug flair.** Radiance, the Glimmer Ward, Provoke (the Grand Facet is the main character), Hold Fast.

**Weak at:** numbers. Few bodies means few objectives held, and every loss hurts. Blasts against big squads don't matter to them, but nothing makes up for a dead elite.

**Belongs here**
- Keywords: armoured, infantry, flying, monster.
- Abilities: Blink, Recall Beam, Radiance, Refraction, Bubble Shield, Provoke, Glimmer Field.
- Supports: recharge, aimBuff, teleport, brace, blast (Sunbeam), the Glimmer Ward summon, and the Grand Portal airdrop.
- Stats: hit 3+; shields 1–3 per model; armour 4–5 (Facet 3); nerve 8–9; cost 3–7, highest per model; precision 1–2 and tracking 1 on most guns.

**Arrival:** the **Grand Portal** beams a reserve unit in within 4" of one of your units (at least 3" from enemies) from round 2, arriving with +1 shield per model for the round. It is in both the Skirmish and Assault starter supports: a few elites need it to reach the long board's many objectives.

**Overlap:** the Prism Guard and Mirage Dancers fight well in melee; the Sunbeam is a strike from orbit, not a gunline.
